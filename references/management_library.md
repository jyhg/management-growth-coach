# Management Library

This starter library helps choose relevant frameworks. It is organized by historical development stage, core school or domain, application domain, and type: theory, methodology, philosophy, or tool.

Do not dump the whole library into an answer. Choose 1-3 relevant frameworks and turn them into concrete questions, actions, or templates.

## Classical Management

Historical development stage: 1890s-1930s.

Core school: classical management.

Application domain: efficiency, standardization, organization design.

- Scientific management. Type: theory and methodology. Representatives: Frederick Taylor, Frank and Lillian Gilbreth. Use for work standardization, time study, motion study, workflow improvement, and incentive design.
- General administrative theory. Type: theory. Representative: Henri Fayol. Use for planning, organizing, commanding, coordinating, and controlling. Tools include unity of command, scalar chain, and departmentalization.
- Bureaucratic organization. Type: theory and philosophy. Representative: Max Weber. Use for formal authority, clear hierarchy, rules, career paths, and role clarity.

## Behavioral Science

Historical development stage: 1930s-1950s.

Core school: behavioral science and human relations.

Application domain: motivation, morale, leadership, group behavior.

- Human relations. Type: theory. Representative: Elton Mayo. Use when informal organization, morale, and relationships affect productivity.
- Maslow hierarchy of needs. Type: theory. Use for diagnosing unmet needs: physiological, safety, belonging, esteem, self-actualization.
- Herzberg two-factor theory. Type: theory. Use to separate hygiene factors from motivators.
- McGregor Theory X and Theory Y. Type: philosophy. Use to inspect assumptions about human nature and autonomy.
- Vroom expectancy theory. Type: theory. Use to check valence, expectancy, and instrumentality.
- Adams equity theory. Type: theory. Use when perceived fairness affects motivation.
- Lewin group dynamics. Type: theory and methodology. Use for group norms, communication, cohesion, and change force analysis.
- Managerial grid. Type: tool. Use to inspect concern for people and concern for production.
- Leadership continuum. Type: methodology. Use to choose between directive and participative leadership.

## Modern Management Theory

Historical development stage: 1950s-1980s.

Core school: management theory jungle.

Application domain: process, systems, decision, contingency, experience, quantitative management.

- Management process school. Type: methodology. Use plan-organize-lead-control loops.
- Social systems school. Type: theory. Representative: Chester Barnard. Use for cooperation systems, communication, authority acceptance, and goal alignment.
- Decision theory school. Type: theory and tool. Representative: Herbert Simon. Use bounded rationality, satisficing, decision tree, Delphi, nominal group technique, fishbone diagram, and 5Why.
- Systems management school. Type: theory and methodology. Use input-output models, subsystem coordination, and whole-system optimization.
- Contingency theory. Type: theory and methodology. Use when no single best management style fits. Tools include Fiedler model, situational leadership, and path-goal theory.
- Empirical or case school. Type: methodology. Representatives: Peter Drucker, Ernest Dale. Use MBO, case analysis, and benchmarking.
- Management science or quantitative school. Type: methodology and tool. Use linear programming, queuing theory, inventory models, simulation, and PERT.

## Strategy Management

Historical development stage: 1980s-present.

Core school: contemporary strategy.

Application domain: competition, growth, strategic execution.

- Competitive strategy. Type: theory and methodology. Representative: Michael Porter. Use cost leadership, differentiation, focus, five forces, value chain, diamond model, and activity system map.
- Classic strategic tools. Type: tool. Use SWOT, TOWS, BCG matrix, GE matrix, SPACE matrix, Ansoff matrix, and McKinsey 7S.
- Core competence. Type: theory. Representatives: Prahalad and Hamel. Use capability maps and core competence identification.
- Blue ocean strategy. Type: methodology. Use strategy canvas, four actions framework, value curve, and non-customer analysis.
- Strategic execution. Type: methodology and tool. Use OGSM, strategy decoding, personal business commitment, and strategic review.

## Operations And Quality Management

Historical development stage: 1980s-present.

Core school: operations and quality management.

Application domain: process efficiency, quality, waste reduction, reliability.

- Total quality management. Type: philosophy and methodology. Representatives: Deming, Juran, Crosby. Use customer focus, PDCA, Juran trilogy, QC seven tools, and new QC seven tools.
- Six Sigma. Type: methodology and tool. Use DMAIC, DMADV, SPC, FMEA, MSA, DOE, Poka-Yoke, and QFD.
- Lean management. Type: philosophy and methodology. Use TPS, waste elimination, 5S/6S/7S, kanban, value stream mapping, JIT, SMED, Andon, one-piece flow, pull system, standard work, Kaizen, 5Why, and value engineering.
- Process management. Type: methodology and tool. Use BPMN, RACI, process maturity assessment, end-to-end process mapping, and process dashboards.

## Organization And HR Management

Historical development stage: 1980s-present.

Core school: organization and human resource management.

Application domain: goals, performance, learning, talent, team.

- Management by objectives. Type: methodology. Representative: Peter Drucker. Use goal decomposition, joint goal setting, self-control, and result orientation.
- Balanced scorecard. Type: methodology and tool. Representatives: Kaplan and Norton. Use financial, customer, internal process, learning and growth dimensions, strategy map, and causal chain.
- OKR. Type: methodology. Use objectives, measurable key results, focus, transparency, alignment, and review.
- Learning organization. Type: philosophy and methodology. Representative: Peter Senge. Use five disciplines, world cafe, open space, and action learning.
- Business process reengineering. Type: methodology. Representatives: Hammer and Champy. Use radical process redesign for breakthrough performance.
- Z theory. Type: philosophy. Representative: William Ouchi. Use trust, subtlety, and long-term employment.
- Amoeba management. Type: methodology and tool. Representative: Kazuo Inamori. Use unit-time accounting, small autonomous units, and internal transfer pricing.
- HR tools. Type: tool. Use BEI, STAR, competency model, assessment center, KPI, 360 feedback, forced distribution, performance interview, nine-box talent review, broadband pay, equity incentive, total rewards, ADDIE, Kirkpatrick evaluation, mentoring, and rotation.
- Team tools. Type: tool. Use Belbin team roles, Tuckman team stages, team charter, and retrospective.
- ASK talent enablement model. Type: methodology. Use attitude, skill, and knowledge as three development levers. Combine selection, value assessment, role model stories, rotation, authorization, mentor pairing, OJT, exams, case analysis, and skill maps.
- IDP personal development plan. Type: tool. Use to align strengths, development direction, target actions, resources, methods, and expected results.
- 3+1 performance goal model. Type: methodology. Use key performance outputs, work tasks, improvement and innovation, and personal capability development.
- New employee six key moments. Type: tool. Use offer acceptance, first day, first week, first month, first mistake, and first excellent performance to design onboarding support.
- Q12-style engagement survey. Type: tool. Use to inspect expectations, support, strengths, recognition, care, growth, voice, mission, quality, trust, progress, and learning.

## Innovation And Change Management

Historical development stage: 1980s-present.

Core school: innovation and change.

Application domain: product innovation, transformation, adoption, resistance.

- Agile management. Type: methodology. Use Scrum, product backlog, sprint, daily standup, review, retrospective, kanban, XP, SAFe, LeSS, user story, burndown chart, sprint planning, and product roadmap.
- Design thinking. Type: methodology. Use empathy, define, ideate, prototype, test, empathy map, journey map, persona, storyboard, and experience map.
- Change management. Type: methodology and tool. Use Kotter 8 steps, force field analysis, stakeholder analysis, change impact assessment, communication plan, change curve, and ADKAR.
- Innovation tools. Type: tool. Use brainstorming, reverse brainstorming, six thinking hats, SCAMPER, mind map, idea evaluation matrix, feasibility analysis, prototype testing, Stage-Gate, open innovation, lean startup, MVP, and business model canvas.

## Project Management

Historical development stage: contemporary integrated management.

Core school: project management.

Application domain: delivery, coordination, schedule, cost, risk.

- PMBOK and PRINCE2. Type: methodology. Use initiation, planning, execution, monitoring, closing, and knowledge areas.
- Project tools. Type: tool. Use Gantt chart, critical path method, critical chain project management, earned value management, WBS, RACI, risk register, project charter, and project management plan.

## Risk Management

Historical development stage: contemporary integrated management.

Core school: risk and internal control.

Application domain: uncertainty, control, crisis, continuity.

- COSO risk management. Type: methodology. Use enterprise risk management elements.
- Crisis management 4R. Type: methodology. Use reduction, readiness, response, recovery.
- Risk tools. Type: tool. Use risk matrix, risk assessment form, contingency plan, business continuity plan, SWOT risk analysis, and fault tree analysis.

## Communication And Collaboration

Historical development stage: contemporary integrated management.

Core school: communication and collaboration.

Application domain: meetings, influence, stakeholder alignment, conflict.

- Meeting management. Type: tool. Use Robert's Rules of Order, agenda template, meeting minutes template, and timeboxing.
- Communication tools. Type: tool. Use nonviolent communication, Johari window, pyramid principle, and elevator pitch.
- Collaboration tools. Type: tool. Use WBS, RACI, kanban, daily standup, stakeholder matrix, and shared-credit design.
- Cross-team collaboration checklist. Type: tool. Use decision maker, contact person, stakeholder benefits and losses, compensation, meaning, recognition, difficulty, doubts, multiple options, KPI intersection, timing, trusted third party, and shared credit.
- Positive feedback five-step method. Type: methodology. Define topic, describe facts, invite cause discussion, connect impact, and elevate motivation.
- Negative feedback five-step method. Type: methodology. Define topic, describe facts, describe short and long-term impact, analyze causes together, and agree improvement plan and support.
- Emotional conflict handling checklist. Type: tool. Pause, restore energy, apologize or lighten tone when appropriate, externalize the problem, use third-party support, create a small action loop, and maintain emotional accounts in normal times.

## Problem Solving And Decision Making

Historical development stage: contemporary integrated management.

Core school: structured problem solving.

Application domain: root cause, decision, prioritization, analysis.

- Structured problem solving. Type: methodology. Use McKinsey seven-step problem solving, pyramid principle, MECE, and logic tree.
- Root cause analysis. Type: tool. Use 5Why, fishbone diagram, fault tree analysis, and FMEA.
- Decision tools. Type: tool. Use decision tree, decision matrix, cost-benefit analysis, SWOT, Delphi, and nominal group technique.
- External perspective checklist. Type: tool. Use essence, historical precedent, boss view, peer view, subordinate impact, customer impact, and KPI alignment to avoid local optimum.
- 10-10-10 reflection. Type: tool. Use 10 seconds, 10 months, and 10 years to inspect short, medium, and long-term consequences.
- Blue-team challenge. Type: methodology. Assign a challenger to test assumptions, create constructive conflict, then align after the decision.
- Premortem reflection. Type: methodology. Before action, imagine failure and identify causes and countermeasures.

## Execution And Review Tools

Historical development stage: contemporary integrated management.

Core school: execution, review, and continuous improvement.

Application domain: goal decomposition, task closure, operating cadence, management inspection.

- 415 rule. Type: methodology. Use 40% effort before the meeting, 10% during alignment, and 50% after the meeting for follow-up and closure.
- Action plan decomposition table. Type: tool. Use key task, owner, collaborator, checker, start and end time, output document, communication mechanism, needed resources, risks, countermeasures, result commitment, and reward or consequence.
- Task decomposition methods. Type: tool. Use step method, element method, formula method, and researched brainstorming.
- Green-cross calendar. Type: tool. Use visible daily execution tracking.
- Task closure rate table. Type: tool. Use to inspect task completion and closure quality.
- Rotating inspection mechanism. Type: methodology. Use transparent, high-frequency checks to inspect work and help people.
- Task retrospective improvement table. Type: tool. Use original goal, current result, variance, success factors, failure factors, keep, improve, start, and stop.

## Leadership And Motivation Tools

Historical development stage: contemporary leadership practice.

Core school: leadership behavior, enablement, and non-compensation motivation.

Application domain: influence, trust, motivation, delegation, team energy.

- Leadership vs management distinction. Type: philosophy. Management focuses on How, SOP, method, and efficiency. Leadership focuses on Why, mission, trust, change, and meaning.
- Circle of influence and circle of concern. Type: tool. Use to move energy from complaint to controllable action.
- Internal vs external attribution. Type: philosophy and self-check. Use to inspect whether the manager is taking responsibility before blaming the environment.
- Responsibility response pattern. Type: methodology. State result and impact, own missing management actions, give countermeasures, and commit to next result.
- Five-point delegation method. Type: methodology. Clarify starting point, ending point, milestones, blockers, and focus.
- Psychological needs ranking. Type: tool. Use to identify individual motivation differences before choosing incentives.
- Wow moment. Type: philosophy and tool. Create unexpected positive moments after hygiene factors are stable.
- Veteran employee motivation. Type: methodology. Use urgency, respect, and freshness.
- New employee activation. Type: methodology. Use ownership, emotional value, and circle identity.
- Game achievement incentive. Type: tool. Use challenging goals, frequent feedback, multidimensional recognition, team competition, and surprise.
- Team-building design dimensions. Type: tool. Use vulnerability, emotion, laughter, physical energy, and belonging.

## Contemporary Frontier Ideas

Historical development stage: present.

Core school: frontier management.

Application domain: digital transformation, sustainability, ecosystems, resilience.

- Servant leadership. Type: philosophy. Representative: Robert Greenleaf. Use when leadership should serve growth, trust, and enablement.
- Platform management. Type: methodology. Use platform business model canvas and ecosystem map.
- ESG management. Type: methodology and tool. Use ESG reporting, ESG rating, and carbon footprint accounting.
- Digital transformation management. Type: methodology. Use digital maturity assessment, data governance framework, and digital twin.
- Resilience management. Type: methodology and tool. Use resilience assessment, scenario planning, and stress testing.
