## Personal Reminders

- Start upward communication with conclusion, plan, responsibility, timeline, and expected result.
- Use background only after the listener understands the proposed action.
- When team issues appear, ask "What pressure, role ambiguity, or emotional depletion did I miss?"
- When cross-team collaboration stalls, first reduce the other side's complexity and offer options.
- When a governance task succeeds, translate it into business value, user experience, or operating impact.
