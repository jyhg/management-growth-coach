# Personal Context

## Current Growth Theme Template

Use this file as a private working template. Before publishing a customized copy, remove organization names, product names, project names, personal timelines, and identifiable team details.

The user's current management transition may include:

- from function-only thinking to business value thinking
- from after-the-fact problem handling to early risk sensing
- from explaining reasons to giving conclusion, plan, owner, timeline, and commitment
- from only checking delivery results to also protecting people, role boundaries, pressure, and collaboration cost
- from one-off delivery to mechanisms, talent development, and repeatable team execution

## Example 30/60/90 Direction

30 days:

- build one small business knowledge-base or management-process sample
- interview key stakeholders, delivery owners, and primary users
- create a simple metric, responsibility, or workflow map
- do team 1:1s on pressure, blockers, responsibility boundary, growth direction, and mechanism suggestions
- maintain member feedback closure and key task tracking

60 days:

- summarize top data problems and improvement plans
- replicate the sample method to another business or team module
- explore AI support for demand decomposition, SQL generation, tests, review, and launch checks
- review common team problems as role boundary, process, or capability issues

90 days:

- expand from one-off support to reusable assets, dashboards, workflows, or playbooks
- form AI development workflow standards
- connect prior operational experience with knowledge assets and AI-assisted workflows
- report business understanding, delivery efficiency, data quality, and team closure improvements

## Personal Reminders

- Start upward communication with conclusion, plan, responsibility, timeline, and expected result.
- Use background only after the listener understands the proposed action.
- When team issues appear, ask "What pressure, role ambiguity, or emotional depletion did I miss?"
- When cross-team collaboration stalls, first reduce the other side's complexity and offer options.
- When an operational task succeeds, translate it into business value, user experience, or operating impact.
