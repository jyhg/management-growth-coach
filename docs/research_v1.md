# Research Notes v1

## Local Source Material

The training notes emphasize:

- leadership vs management
- system thinking
- proactive responsibility
- talent enablement
- MBO and project execution
- retrospective discipline
- cross-team collaboration
- recognition, motivation, and emotional resources
- delegation and non-compensation incentives

The 1:1 report emphasizes the user's personal growth direction:

- deepen business understanding
- better protect team member boundaries and pressure
- change upward reporting from explanation-first to solution-first

## External Frameworks To Use Carefully

These frameworks are useful as optional lenses, not rigid answers:

- DISC behavioral tendencies
- MBO / SMART goals
- Gallup Q12 engagement questions
- psychological safety
- SBI or fact-impact-action style feedback
- situational leadership
- 5 Whys
- stakeholder mapping

## Research Rules

When browsing:

- prefer official, academic, or established management sources
- cite URLs in the answer
- avoid long quotes
- use external theory only to sharpen the user's current decision

## Known Risk

Management advice can become performative. The skill must repeatedly pull the user back to:

- facts
- responsibility
- stakeholder impact
- next observable action
- follow-up date
